Pairing Your Phone

The ZeroAgent companion app for iPhone watches your workers from wherever you are. It needs a Mac running ZeroAgent with the relay connected, and it pairs by scanning a QR code:

  1. On your Mac, open ZeroAgent, sign in with GitHub, and open the account panel's "Show QR code."
  2. On your phone, open the ZeroAgent app and scan the code the Mac is showing.
  3. The phone is now paired to that account and shows what your Mac sends to the relay.

The relay is a Premium feature. See the Privacy Policy for what it carries while it is on, and how to turn it off.
